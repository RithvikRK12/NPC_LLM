# Natural acceptance of the inspection offer

The exact reply “Sure! That would be helpful.” previously missed the Craftsman intent adapter's exact-string agreement list. It therefore produced social speech rather than a verified begin-inspection operation; the client had no accepted outing to close chat for.

Acceptance now recognizes short affirmative clauses with courtesy clauses and punctuation, including the exact report, “Yes, thank you!”, “That would be great, thanks.” and “I'd appreciate that.” Every clause must be assent or courtesy. An outstanding inspection offer is still required. Quotes, hypotheticals, negations, postponed help, different subjects and unrelated text do not acquire authority from a leading “sure.” The existing capability checks, operation transaction, receipts, idempotency and client transition remain unchanged.
