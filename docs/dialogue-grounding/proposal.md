# Dialogue grounding proposal

Date: 2026-10-20 (Asia/Kolkata). Status: requirements proposal; product decisions D1–D3 confirmed by the user on 2026-10-20. No solution design or implementation is authorized.

This document defines the desired behavior and observable acceptance criteria for Craftsman and Gatherer dialogue. It does not select an architecture, prescribe prompt changes, design schemas or algorithms, or authorize implementation, model replacement, deployment, or a release-gate change.

## Problem statement

Players need to trust what villagers say about Willowbrook, themselves, the player, and events in the game. The current production conversation path can publish unsupported factual claims even when the correct authored fact is supplied. It also omits ordinary public village knowledge, loses character constraints on some prompt paths, selects stale conversation turns, fails to produce durable memories from ordinary disclosures, and sometimes rejects harmless uncertainty or figurative language. Ownership and perspective errors make NPC belongings, work, or preferences become the player's, and vice versa.

These failures coexist with deterministic gameplay authority. An NPC can falsely say it gave the player axes without actually transferring anything. Preserving execution safety is necessary, but does not make the published dialogue truthful. The desired product combines reliable factual content with expressive, distinct characters; a universal generic fallback would not satisfy that goal.

## Evidence and confidence

The primary evidence is the [audit README](../evidence/dialogue-grounding-audit-2026-10-20/README.md), [live transcripts](../evidence/dialogue-grounding-audit-2026-10-20/results.md), and their captured production-worker inputs and outputs. The audit used disposable seeded SQLite worlds, bypassed HTTP/Godot presentation, and exercised production context, generation, validation, and finalization. Its 20 live scenarios contain 22 accepted turns: 17 generated and five fallback replies. These are diagnostic examples, not quality-rate or latency acceptance measurements. All worlds retained their starting player inventory; no transfer receipts or player-report memory rows were produced.

The current working-tree backend was inspected alongside the evidence. Relevant files are linked below; their existing local changes are part of the investigation baseline, not changes made by this proposal.

### Hypotheses and limits

- Generic ship-specific instructions may prime unsolicited ship dialogue. Their presence and the unsupported offer are observed; the audit does not isolate a causal effect.
- Repair, routing and context differences may amplify the model's errors. Their individual contributions and failure frequency require controlled evaluation; the evidence does not establish that the current Llama is incapable of the requirements.
- Semantic retrieval is disabled in the audited configuration, and the [master progress record](../redesign-master-progress.md) says the production FAISS connection remains proposed. Retrieval improvements alone cannot recover disclosures that were never persisted. No retrieval technology is selected here.
- The history-poison follow-up ended in `INTERNAL_FAILURE`. It is not evidence that a grounding guard successfully corrected the prior reply, nor is its underlying failure diagnosed here.
- Injected cases measure containment, not naturally occurring hallucination frequency. The live sample does not establish broad release readiness, production PostgreSQL dialogue quality, or Godot presentation behavior.

## Goals

1. Make published dialogue consistent with authored world knowledge, stable character identity, current authoritative state, and compatible accepted conversation history.
2. Make public village knowledge available to both NPCs while preserving the distinction between public, personal, disclosed, observed and server-only knowledge.
3. Maintain recent-turn continuity and useful durable, attributed recall after the transcript window expires.
4. Answer equivalent and mixed questions reliably without flattening either character's voice.
5. Contain unsupported factual assertions and preserve safe uncertainty, supported paraphrase, humor, feelings and figurative speech.
6. Preserve all deterministic gameplay authority, lifecycle rules and existing release-quality gates.

## Non-goals

- Designing or implementing a solution, selecting validators/retrievers, changing wire contracts, or defining an implementation sequence.
- Switching or fine-tuning models, using hosted inference, adding an online semantic judge, or implicitly adding inference calls.
- Expanding the roster, map, biography, weather simulation, services, quests, ship repair, sailing or ending to make invented dialogue true.
- General unrestricted worldbuilding, automatic cross-NPC gossip, or granting generated canon mechanical authority. Existing compatible accepted narrative continuity remains respected; its production is not expanded here.
- Perfect open-domain truth verification, a canned response for every paraphrase, or elimination of all fallbacks.
- Save migration/reset, unrelated refactoring, changing rollout status, or closing quality gates from this document.

## Users and scenarios

| User | Scenario | Desired experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New player | Asks the village name, who lives here, and where to find the other NPC. | Learns Willowbrook, the authored public roster and usual places; receives no fabricated people, live sighting or route. |
| Curious player | Asks “What do you do?”, “What is your trade?”, or asks about the Craftsman's father. | Gets the same supported occupation in natural wording and a biography answer that respects authored limits. |
| Distressed player | Says they are scared and lost, then asks the village name and the NPC's work. | Receives emotional acknowledgment plus both practical answers, without an unsolicited undisclosed ship topic. |
| Returning player | Discloses a preference, discusses other topics, restarts the backend, then asks what they said. | Gets useful attributed recall outside the visible transcript window, retaining corrections and uncertainty. |
| Player testing boundaries | Supplies a false village name, claims a past transfer, or requests unsupported training. | Receives a grounded correction, limitation or clarification. No false event, new service or gameplay effect results. |
| Player exchanging items | Asks about belongings, requests a transfer, or thanks an NPC after inspection. | Hears correct owners, transfer direction and actual progress; accepted inspection never becomes completed repair. |
| Content author / maintainer | Changes authored knowledge or investigates an awkward reply. | Can assess which knowledge and history were available and why the final reply was accepted, repaired or degraded. |

## Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| F1 | Every published reply, including composition, repair and fallback prose, must respect factual support in the game's bounded domains: world/NPC identity, biography constraints, locations, inventories/quantities/ownership, recipes, supported services, quest/activity progress and completed actions. Unsupported facts must not become publishable merely through missing references, plausible prose, quoted history or model-supplied evidence IDs. |
| F2 | Both NPCs must know the authored public village name, roster, work descriptions and known places. A usual location must remain a tendency; a current location or witnessed event requires applicable observation. A place name supplies no route, schedule or guiding capability. An incomplete catalog must not justify claims of exhaustive knowledge beyond what authors establish. |
| F3 | Stable occupation, identity and authored prohibitions must govern all dialogue paths, including greetings, ordinary speech, action reactions, repair and fallback. Optional detail selection and context pressure must not silently erase these constraints. Supported work does not automatically grant training, tools, stock or consent. |
| F4 | Conversation continuity must follow finalized turn sequence in the same scope, NPC and epoch. Recent context must prefer the newest eligible turns and present retained turns chronologically. It must include accepted fallback exchanges where relevant, exclude rejected drafts, and resolve short follow-ups against the latest applicable topic. Current messages and mandatory knowledge must not be displaced by old dialogue. |
| F5 | Eligible ordinary disclosures must produce durable attributed recall through the production conversation path, not only manually seeded retrieval. Memory must identify whose statement it was, preserve uncertainty and accepted source provenance, and survive transcript-window eviction and process restart. Relevant preferences, personal circumstances and corrections are eligible automatically, without an explicit request to remember (D2). Questions, hypothetical/quoted claims and rejected drafts must not become asserted personal facts. |
| F6 | Explicit corrections and retractions must supersede the relevant personal recollection without reviving stale assertions. Recalled player claims must remain claims and cannot verify inventory, transfers, quest progress or NPC-witnessed events. Clear/reset and NPC/scope isolation must retain their current semantics. |
| F7 | The product must distinguish public authored knowledge, this NPC's personal lore, witnessed/disclosed information, attributed player reports, accepted narrative continuity and privileged execution state. Server truth alone must not establish that an NPC knows it unless product policy explicitly permits that knowledge. Both villagers always know the player's full current inventory without requiring observation or disclosure (D1); this permission does not extend to other privileged state. Execution checks must retain full authoritative state regardless. |
| F8 | Preserve owner, subject, speaker, addressee, action direction and event status in answers. “I” refers to the speaking NPC and “you” to the player. A player's preference must not overwrite NPC taste; one NPC's private biography or conversation must not appear as another's firsthand knowledge. |
| F9 | Answer the user's actual concerns, including emotional and practical parts. Occupation paraphrases and personal-memory questions must receive relevant answers rather than action-request instructions solely because wording differs. Ambiguous supported actions must still clarify before execution. |
| F10 | Explicit uncertainty, ordinary metaphor, opinion and supported factual paraphrase must remain acceptable. Uncertainty cannot excuse smuggling in invented details, such as naming an unsupported elder or location behind “perhaps.” Unknown current conditions and unauthored history must not be completed by invention; biography gaps must receive a brief in-character boundary without inventing an answer or a reason for withholding it (D3). |
| F11 | False premises and incompatible earlier NPC assertions must not rewrite authored identity or current mechanics. Correct the relevant misconception naturally. A conversational recollection proves that something was said, not that the asserted event occurred. |
| F12 | Offers, promises, accepted activities and completed outcomes must remain distinct. Unsupported services must not be promised. Narration, memory and generated canon must never authorize effects, create consent, invent receipts, or imply that a failed/unfinished operation completed. |

## Non-functional requirements and preserved constraints

- **N1 — Local inference:** retain the current local Llama model, audited as `llama3:willowbrook`; [model evidence](../evidence/dialogue-grounding-audit-2026-10-20/models.json) records its digest. No hosted inference or replacement model is authorized. Installed alternatives are not permission to switch. Any future additional inference or changed runtime budget requires explicit consideration in subsequent design and latency acceptance.
- **N2 — Save and workspace safety:** development/evaluation must use fresh disposable worlds and databases, preserve both existing `mvp.db` saves and unrelated local work, and write new evidence without overwriting the audit. This proposal changes documentation only.
- **N3 — Authority and recovery:** retain per-NPC durable ordering, cross-NPC concurrency rules, at most one mutation per turn, current-question consent, no timer/turn negotiation expiry, authoritative rechecks, atomic effects/receipts, separate atomic final speech/continuity, idempotent delivery and receipt-safe recovery/rollback. Never undo committed effects because speech fails.
- **N4 — Bounded operation:** preserve the existing request/output/context/deadline/queue/lease policies, one ordinary proposal attempt and shared single repair allowance. No provider, embedding or semantic-check call may run under a database lock/transaction. No silent truncation; readable beats must preserve complete final dialogue. Safe degradation must remain available.
- **N5 — Character quality:** retain the Craftsman's warm practicality and restrained dry humor and the Gatherer's authored voice, beliefs and uncertainty. Emotional acknowledgment and figurative language must remain possible without invented mechanics or compulsory anecdotes. Dialogue must not expose internal IDs, source bookkeeping or validation terminology.
- **N6 — Observability:** evaluation must distinguish raw model mistakes, validation misses, false-positive rejections, repairs, fallbacks, final factual correctness and gameplay effects. Record scenario/turn denominators, model/configuration, knowledge availability, memory provenance and ordinary/action/repair latency distributions. A generated origin is not a correctness score; a fallback is not automatically a relevant answer.
- **N7 — Release discipline:** preserve the frozen scenario evaluation and all mechanical, isolated SQLite/PostgreSQL, API, Godot lifecycle/retry and rollback gates applicable to subsequent changes. The [master progress record](../redesign-master-progress.md) reports engineering integration separately from open T03 numerical quality/product-latency acceptance and dependent T04 broad rollout. Its recorded 1,150 backend tests, eight PostgreSQL runners and 21 Godot scenarios are historical baseline evidence, not fresh verification of this proposal or future work. The existing human-review waiver remains in force; no new reviewer requirement is introduced.

## Assumptions and unresolved product decisions

The authored catalog and profiles are the source of identity and public lore for this scope; gaps remain gaps. Current mechanical truth overrides stale mechanical recollections; authored constraints override conflicting generated narrative. Compatible accepted conversation remains part of the player's experience. Personal memory is scoped to the receiving NPC and current conversation epoch; existing clear removes conversational payloads while preserving accepted mechanics/receipts and generated canon, and new game changes scope and resets run-specific state/canon. No automatic knowledge sharing is assumed.

These are behavioral requirements, not a mandate for a particular rendering, extraction, memory or validation mechanism. The user confirmed the following product decisions on 2026-10-20:

| ID | Confirmed decision | Requirement and acceptance impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Full current player inventory is always visible to both villagers. | No showing, disclosure or exchange is required. F7 and A9 must permit accurate current-inventory answers while preserving ownership and freshness. This exception does not grant knowledge of other privileged server state or prove past transfers. |
| D2 | Relevant preferences, personal circumstances and corrections are remembered durably from ordinary disclosures. | No special remember command is required. Retention remains bounded by current lifecycle rules; this does not authorize storing every utterance indefinitely. F5 and A6 cover automatic production, attributed recall and corrections. |
| D3 | Use a brief in-character boundary when authored biography leaves a fact unspecified. | Do not invent the answer, trauma, estrangement, forgetfulness or another explanation for the gap. F10 and A4 require natural boundary language without exposing authoring bookkeeping. |

Existing numerical quality thresholds remain fixed. Numerical playable-latency and readable-length product acceptance are already open T03 decisions; this proposal does not invent values, choose new budgets, or ask to reconfirm established audit facts.

Inspect actual outgoing context and final published speech, persisted history/memory and authoritative before/after state.
